# Echo’s Cry — Feel the Beat

- **Play with a controller!** Controller support now covers combat, movement, items, dialogue, and menus, with button prompts that follow your input device.
- **Find your rhythm more easily.** Clearer musical beats and a redesigned timing bar help you know when to strike.
- **Feel every Excellent hit.** Nail the timing to hear a crisp drum crack that adds punch to your attacks.
- **Keep your groove going.** More forgiving timing and a smaller combo penalty make it easier to recover from a missed beat.
- **Hit harder and stay in the fight longer.** Increased starting health and stronger Clarinet and Drum attacks give you more fighting power.
- **Spend less time chipping away at enemies.** Bats and slimes are less durable.
- **Read dialogue without getting ambushed.** Combat now pauses during conversations.
- **Get a closer view of the action.** The camera now sits closer to the player.
- **Use items with a quick D-pad press.** Controller item use is more direct, with a brief highlight showing which slot you used.
- **Navigate menus more comfortably.** Clear controller highlights, separate keyboard and controller binding lists, and smoother navigation make settings and upgrades easier to use.
- **Get clearer combat feedback.** Improved beat tracking and special-attack timing, plus fixes for overlapping hit ratings, make your results easier to read.
- **See your remaining heat correctly.** Upgraded heat bars now show the heat you actually have. Special attacks cost 6 points and leave any extra heat available.
- **See your new dash effect right away.** Its icon now appears as soon as you use the item and shows when the effect becomes active.
- **Enjoy a tidier HUD.** Fixed incorrect player-level displays, unreliable upgrade descriptions, and duplicate menu selection markers.
